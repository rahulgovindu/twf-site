## 1. What this is

**The Way Forward** (thewayforward.in) is a platform for UPSC and government
exam aspirants rebuilding their careers after their attempts end. Founded by
**Rahul Govindu** (Hyderabad), an ex-UPSC aspirant (6+ years, 17,500+ hours,
never made the final list) who rebuilt through entrepreneurship and now helps
others do the same.

**Phase 1 (now):** landing site to attract visitors and build an audience
(email list + WhatsApp + podcast guest pipeline).
**Phase 2 (~November 2026, ±90 days from Aug 2026):** a portal offering
services to aspirants — stories/podcast, curated jobs, skill tracks,
one-on-one support. Newsletter subscribers get early access.

## 2. Mission, positioning, audience

- **Mission:** reduce post-UPSC trauma; give aspirants clarity, career
  direction, and their confidence back.
- **Positioning:** NOT coaching, NOT therapy, NOT a job consultancy. A
  platform by someone who lived the failure — "what I searched for in my
  darkest month and couldn't find."
- **Audience:** UPSC/government-exam aspirants in late attempts, out of
  attempts, or carrying the weight after moving on. Secondary: their parents;
  ex-aspirants who succeeded (podcast guests).
- **Signature concept:** **POST-UPSC Syndrome** — coined by Rahul. Definition
  (use verbatim): "the phase after your final attempts when you realise the
  dream may never come true — and you must rebuild your identity, career and
  confidence, often carrying a multi-year gap." Always add: "A lived
  experience, not a medical diagnosis." Casing: POST-UPSC Syndrome.

## 9. Forms & backend (Hostinger)

- Hosting: Hostinger Web Host Business (shared; PHP + MySQL, no Node).
- Endpoints in `hostinger-api/`: `subscribe.php` (newsletter →
  `subscribers` table), `book-call.php` (call requests → `call_requests`).
  Both: prepared statements, email validation, honeypot field `website`,
  same-origin check, generic errors. `config.php` (DB creds) is gitignored
  AND denied by `hostinger-api/.htaccess`.
- `public/.htaccess` (copied into out/): HTTPS redirect, security headers,
  HSTS, compression, caching.
- **Deploy:** `npm run build`; upload `out/` contents → `public_html/`;
  upload `hostinger-api/` PHP + .htaccess → `public_html/api/`; one-time:
  create DB in hPanel, import `schema.sql`, fill `config.php`. Full steps
  in `hostinger-api/README.md`.
- Known accepted gap: no rate limiting (add Cloudflare free tier if abused).
- After deploy: add site to Google Search Console and submit sitemap.
